Following what drifts

The mystery of pollen emerges when the invisible inner structures of nature are revealed through art.

This website follows the quiet movement of pollen as it drifts between flowers, air, and human perception.

Through images, traces, and delicate visual gestures, it translates what is too small to see into something we can sense.

Here, pollen becomes not just a particle, but a hidden structure connecting nature and ourselves.
